Key item features

Seamless Tracking: Effortlessly locate items like keys, wallets, and luggage using the Apple Find My app, integrating with friends' devices for comprehensive tracking.

Instant Setup: Enjoy a quick, one-tap setup that instantly connects the AirTag with your iPhone or iPad for immediate use.

Audible Locator: Utilize the built-in speaker to play a sound for finding nearby items, or simply ask Siri for assistance.

Precision Guidance: Benefit from Precision Finding, which uses Ultra Wideband technology on select iPhone models to guide you directly to your AirTag.

Extended Network: Leverage the vast Find My network, comprising hundreds of millions of Apple devices, to locate items even when they are farther away.

Privacy-Focused Design: All communication within the Find My network is anonymous and encrypted, ensuring privacy with no location data or history stored on the AirTag.

Durable Power: Features a replaceable battery that provides over a year of power and boasts IP67 water and dust resistance for enhanced durability.
