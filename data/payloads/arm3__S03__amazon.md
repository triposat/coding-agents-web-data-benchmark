Since I’m in NYC, there are iPhones everywhere, so my AirTag’s location is updated constantly no matter how far away I am. If you live somewhere more rural or isolated, updates may be less frequent because there are fewer Apple devices nearby to relay its location. Overall I’m so happy with this purchase. It works better than any other tracker I’ve used. I want to get another one to keep into my car so I can find where I parked after a night of bar hopping lol.
