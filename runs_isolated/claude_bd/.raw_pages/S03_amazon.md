I got an AirTag to replace my tile. AirTags don’t have their own GPS, they use nearby iPhones to anonymously report their location to Apple’s Find My network. Since I’m in NYC, there are iPhones everywhere, so my AirTag’s location is updated constantly no matter how far away I am. Overall I’m so happy with this purchase. It works better than any other tracker I’ve used.
